Common Questions

Everything you need to know about PEO services, co-employment, and working with Helpside.

What is a PEO and how is it different from a payroll company?

A Professional Employer Organization enters a co-employment relationship with your business. Helpside handles HR administration, payroll, benefits, and workers compensation under our federal tax ID, while you retain full control of daily operations, hiring, and firing. A payroll company only cuts checks. A PEO manages the whole employment lifecycle.

Do we have to change our current health insurance broker?

No. Helpside works alongside your broker. Many of our clients keep their existing broker relationship while gaining access to our master medical plans and lower rates.

Are we locked into a long term contract?

No. Helpside agreements are month-to-month with 30 days notice. If we are not the right fit, you can leave.

How does co-employment affect our business?

You keep full control over hiring, firing, day-to-day management, and business strategy. Helpside becomes the employer of record for tax filings and benefits administration. Your employees remain your employees in every meaningful sense.

Will our employees have to change their health insurance?

Employees will move to Helpside master medical, dental, and vision plans during your next open enrollment. Most employees see better plan options and lower premiums after the switch.

How long does the onboarding process take?

Our standard implementation is 30 days from signed agreement to first payroll on the Helpside system. Complex cases with multi-state operations or executive benefits can take 45 to 60 days.

What states does Helpside serve?

Helpside actively serves clients in Utah, Idaho, Wyoming, Nevada, Colorado, Kansas, Missouri, Tennessee, and Texas. We can support businesses in additional states on request.

Do you help with workers compensation claims?

Yes. Workers compensation coverage is included in our Growth and Enterprise tiers. We handle claims filing, injury investigation, return-to-work planning, and OSHA reporting.

Can we integrate Helpside with our accounting software?

Yes. Helpside integrates with QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, and most popular general ledger platforms. Payroll journal entries post automatically each cycle.

What happens if we need to lay off employees?

Your dedicated HR consultant walks you through the process from planning through separation. We handle WARN Act notices, severance agreement drafting, unemployment claim response, and COBRA administration.

Is there an employee mobile app?

Yes. Every employee gets access to a mobile app for pay stubs, W-2s, PTO requests, benefits information, and direct deposit management. Included at no extra cost.

How do we get started?

Book a 30 minute conversation through our contact form. No sales pitch, no obligation. If Helpside is a fit, we will send a proposal within a week.
