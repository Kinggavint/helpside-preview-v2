Our Process

Five Steps From First Call to Fully Onboarded.

Switching to a PEO can feel overwhelming. Helpside makes it a coordinated 30 day process with clear checkpoints, real people, and no surprises.

01

Step 01

Discover

We start with a 30 minute conversation. You share what is working, what is broken, and what you need to solve. No sales pitch, just listening.

02

Step 02

Assess

Our team reviews your current payroll setup, benefits plans, HR policies, and compliance posture. You get a written summary of gaps, risks, and quick wins.

03

Step 03

Transition

We handle the switch. Payroll data migration, benefits enrollment, employee communication, and handbook updates all happen in a coordinated 30 day plan.

04

Step 04

Integrate

Your employees log in to a single portal for pay stubs, benefits, PTO requests, and W-2s. Your leadership team gets a named HR consultant on speed dial.

05

Step 05

Grow With Us

Quarterly reviews, annual benefits renewals, and proactive compliance updates. Helpside scales with you as you hire, expand states, or restructure.
