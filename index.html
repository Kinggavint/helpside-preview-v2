Frequently Asked

What Business Owners Ask Us Most.

What is a PEO and how is it different from a payroll company?

A Professional Employer Organization enters a co-employment relationship with your business. Helpside handles HR administration, payroll, benefits, and workers compensation under our federal tax ID, while you retain full control of daily operations, hiring, and firing. A payroll company only cuts checks. A PEO manages the whole employment lifecycle.

Do we have to change our current health insurance broker?

No. Helpside works alongside your broker. Many of our clients keep their existing broker relationship while gaining access to our master medical plans and lower rates.

Are we locked into a long term contract?

No. Helpside agreements are month-to-month with 30 days notice. If we are not the right fit, you can leave.
