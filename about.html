About Helpside

Utah Owned. People Focused. Since 1988.

Helpside was founded to help small and mid-size employers compete for talent, control benefits costs, and stay compliant without hiring an in-house HR department.

Our Story

Three Decades of Getting HR Right.

Helpside was founded in Utah in 1988 with a simple idea: business owners should be spending their time serving customers and building products, not chasing tax filings and interpreting benefit summaries.

Since then, we have grown to support more than 800 businesses across nine states, with a full team of certified HR consultants, payroll specialists, and licensed benefits advisors. All based in the Intermountain West.
