Services

One Team. Six Core Services. Fully Integrated.

Every Helpside client gets access to the full stack. HR, payroll, benefits, risk, retirement, and HR technology. No add-on charges, no separate vendors, no gaps.

HR Outsourcing

Employee handbooks, onboarding workflows, terminations, investigations, and manager training. A dedicated HR consultant handles the hard conversations and keeps you compliant with federal and state employment law.

  • Custom employee handbook development
  • New hire onboarding paperwork and I-9 compliance
  • Performance improvement plans and terminations
  • Workplace investigation support
  • Manager and employee training programs
  • Multi-state compliance monitoring

Payroll Administration

Full service payroll processing with tax filings, garnishments, direct deposit, and year end W-2s. Employees get a modern self service portal and mobile app. Business owners get a single quarterly summary.

  • Weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, or monthly cycles
  • Federal, state, and local tax filings
  • Garnishment and child support processing
  • Direct deposit and paperless pay statements
  • Year end W-2 and 1099 distribution
  • Time and attendance integration

Employee Benefits

Access master medical, dental, vision, and voluntary plans with the buying power of hundreds of businesses. Lower rates, better plan options, and less renewal volatility than shopping alone.

  • Group medical, dental, and vision plans
  • 401(k) and retirement plan administration
  • Life and disability insurance
  • Voluntary benefits and worksite products
  • Section 125 cafeteria plans
  • Open enrollment and communication support

Risk & Safety

Workers compensation coverage, OSHA compliance, safety programs, and claims management. Helpside is your loss control partner from onboarding through injury response.

  • Workers compensation coverage and claims
  • Safety program development
  • OSHA reporting and compliance
  • Injury investigation and return-to-work programs
  • Certificate of insurance issuance
  • Loss control site visits

Retirement Plans

Group 401(k) plans with low administrative fees, employee education, and automatic enrollment options. Helpside handles the plan compliance, so your team can focus on saving.

  • Group 401(k) and Safe Harbor plans
  • Automatic enrollment and escalation
  • Employee education and rollover support
  • Annual plan compliance testing
  • Fiduciary support
  • Access to institutional investment lineups

HR Technology

A single unified portal for employees and administrators. Real time reporting, mobile access, and integration with your accounting or scheduling software.

  • Employee self service portal
  • Manager and admin dashboards
  • PTO and time-off tracking
  • ACA and EEO-1 reporting
  • API integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, and popular scheduling tools
  • Mobile app for pay stubs and W-2s
