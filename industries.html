Industries We Serve

Real Expertise in the Verticals Where HR Gets Hard.

Helpside supports growing companies across eight industries where compliance is complex and margins for error are thin. Every client gets a consultant who understands their world.

Built for Your Business, Your Industry

Sector-specific HR expertise that goes beyond one-size-fits-all.

Construction

Multi-state crews, workers comp complexity, and Davis-Bacon compliance. Helpside handles certified payroll, safety programs, and job cost reporting so contractors can bid and win.

Manufacturing

Shift differentials, piece-rate pay, OSHA compliance, and skilled trades benefits. Our team keeps production running while managing HR risk.

Professional Services

Law, accounting, engineering, and consulting firms need competitive benefits to attract talent. Helpside delivers master plans that rival Fortune 500 packages.

Healthcare

Clinics, dental practices, and specialty providers get HIPAA-compliant HR processes, credentialing support, and healthcare-worker benefits that retain staff.

Hospitality

Restaurants, hotels, and event venues have complex tip credit rules, high turnover, and seasonal staffing. Helpside handles it all with mobile-first onboarding.

Technology

Startups and growing SaaS companies need equity administration, remote-work compliance, and multi-state hiring support. We move at your speed.

Nonprofit

501(c)(3) organizations get grant-friendly cost accounting, board reporting, and mission-aligned benefits packages.

Retail

Multi-location retailers get centralized payroll, scheduling integration, and turnkey compliance across store locations.
